# Target art style

The look the user chose (2026-11-21, from HD-2D reference screenshots of a forest campsite and two town plazas). Judge visual changes in `js/render.js`, `js/post.js` (`GRADE`, depth of field, bloom) and `js/sprites.js` against it. Art stays original and drawn in code: match the style, never copy another game's assets.

- Pixel-art characters and props in a small lit diorama, seen from a high three-quarter camera.
- Strong tilt-shift depth of field: only a band around the action is sharp; foreground and background are heavily blurred.
- A warm, golden, low-key grade with a dark vignette; dappled sunlight patches and soft shadow pools on the ground.
- Dense textured ground (grass and earth noise), foliage framing the edges of the frame, bloom on lights (lanterns, fire), floating dust motes and fireflies.
- Prop clutter around characters (logs, barrels, lanterns, a campfire) makes spaces feel lived in.
- From the town-plaza references: an amber tungsten grade, mostly dim with pools of warm light; lamp posts and strings of lanterns between poles, lit windows; desaturated misty far scenery; dark blurred foliage in the corners.
- Night and weather keep this look: warm lantern pools over a cool fill, wet cobbles reflecting lanterns, warm haze rather than grey fog. Never a flat tint.
